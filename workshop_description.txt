[b]Quick Forage Icon Discard[/b] - Quick discard by single mouse click (RMB by default) instead of context menu

Quickly discard foraged items with a single mouse click instead of opening the context menu. Save time and streamline your foraging experience!

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Quick discard forage items with a single click
[*]Customizable mouse button (RMB by default)
[*]No context menu needed
[/list]

[*]Start playing!

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
